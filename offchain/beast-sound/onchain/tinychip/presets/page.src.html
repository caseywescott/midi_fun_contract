TinyChip presets

All 100 presets of the TinyChip bank, converted to TinySynthSettings timbres for loothero's onchain-midi-player and played through its pinned TinySynth engine (fork 4bf9829), installed the way the player installs settings: sampled chip waves via setSampleWave, one timbre per program slot. The 20 in the launch set have exactly the timbres BeastMidiProvider.get_settings serves (checked against synth_settings.mjs at build). The other 80 were left out of the launch build.

Tick Keep on the ones you want in the optional pack, then open Export pack to copy the program list, a share link, the settings JSON or a gzipped base64 payload.
