The language of the study

A very American set of possibilities. Gershwin’s jazz-age concert music, Van Dyke Parks’s orchestral songcraft, Tin Pan Alley and spacious American concert harmonies meet here. These are original procedural studies of shared musical devices, with no quoted melodies. Choose a texture, then change the harmony, rhythm, orchestration or any of the three seeds.

Chords with somewhere to go. Major ninths and sixths colour the tonic; minor ninths open the predominant; altered dominants and tritone substitutions lean into a resolution. Borrowed minor chords darken a major key. Quartal voicings open up the skyline. The chromatic inner line steps between the third and fifth while syncopated themes and walking bass keep the music moving.

From seed to score. The three seeds independently guide melody, harmony and counterpoint. In Harmonize mode a scored chord path follows your tune. Theme variations preserve the tune while changing its surroundings; A–B–A′ travels to a contrasting key and returns. The motion lane marks contrary (C), oblique (O) and similar (S) movement.
