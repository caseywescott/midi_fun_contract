Music-driven Beasts

Each Beast plays its Species Song, exactly the MIDI its token plays. Today (BeatSync) a GIF steps one frame per eighth note, so it follows the tempo and nothing else. Here the composer's own structure drives the art too: the sections and their keys, each voice's theme entry, the climax and the final cadence, plus every note's velocity. Switch modes while it plays to compare (m cycles the frame modes, e the cue effects). The frame modes change only which GIF frame shows; the cue effects are a separate switch.

    • Theme entries: a pulse and a glow in the entering voice's colour (the theme voice strongest).
    • Sections: the backdrop changes with each section's key area, with a sweep on its downbeat.
    • Climax: a punch, a palette flash and a flash of light, and the frames run on sixteenths for that bar.
    • Cadences: the Beast bows and dims and its frames slow to quarter notes; on the final cadence they slow again and hold still until the loop starts over.
    • Velocity: every note-on lights its voice's meter and the art breathes with the overall energy; every bar line gives a small nudge.
