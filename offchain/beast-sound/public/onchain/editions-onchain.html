Monthly Track Editions, onchain

From edition 1, a track bought for a Beast is played by that edition's style engine, The style is waltz, chorale, lullaby or dirge, taking turns, and it plays the species' theme with the instruments chosen for that style. This page plays exactly what the Beast composer contract returns. The MIDI is byte for byte the contract's, and the instruments are the contract's own settings. Both are installed the way onchain-midi-player installs them.

Calendar

Edition 0's purchases are bought songs from the Beast's own composer, not shown here. From edition 1 the five styles take turns, and each pass through them changes the tempo. Within an edition every purchase rolls its own key, mode, form, progressions, accompaniment, bass and percussion figures, theme opening, phrasing and parts (step through purchases with [ and ]). An edition is a period on the Sound Manager's edition clock; the months here are the editions page's calendar.
