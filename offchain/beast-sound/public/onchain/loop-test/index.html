Loop test pages

Each page below is the exact animation_url HTML returned by MidiSoundPage.token_uri on a local devnet, with the fixed composer (PR #3). Tap to play and listen through the end: after the last note there is an 8-beat rest (the section's closing rest), then the loop starts again on the next downbeat.

  • Tier 5 common: 3 bars of music, then 2 bars of rest (loop 5 bars, about 10 s)
  • Sorrow Peak Warlock: 7 bars, then 2 bars of rest (loop 9 bars, about 18 s)
  • Tier 1 Brute, shiny and animated: 7 bars, then 2 bars of rest, with BeatSync on the animated art

Fresh mints have no kills, so these are single-section forms. Multi-section Beasts (see the BeatSync demo) have the same rest between every section and at the loop.
