A world in miniature. Original generated material, informed by Mahler’s procedures rather than quotations. A seeded falling-second / rising-fourth motive returns as song, dance, lament and chorale. The Symphonic journey passes through a march, a country dance, a night episode, a crisis and a transformed return; Double variations alternates two families of material. These are compact models of large-scale processes, not full symphonies.

Harmony and voice leading. Functional phrases acquire applied dominants, mixture, Neapolitan and augmented-sixth approaches, deceptive expansion and chromatic bass lines. The fate gesture turns a major tonic into minor with its third falling a semitone. Three ordered inner voices are chosen together, rewarding common tones and stepwise travel, penalizing parallel perfect intervals and unresolved leading notes or chordal sevenths. Prepared dissonances resolve downward; accented melodic appoggiaturas resolve by step. The separate countermelody favors contrary and oblique motion. The motion lane reports detected parallels between the two melodic lines.

Listen through the form. Click a section to hear it, or isolate it with Score view. Progressive tonality lets the final region differ from the opening. The farewell ending thins, fragments and slows the theme. Harmonize mode preserves the pitches and timing you enter and scores chord paths beneath them. Save MIDI exports the full score and separate parts, with section markers, key signatures, meter and tempo changes. Playback uses lightweight synthesized orchestral colors; the MIDI is intended for your orchestral instruments.
