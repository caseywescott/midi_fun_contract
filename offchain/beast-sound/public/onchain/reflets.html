Reflets Debussy · Ravel · Mel Bonis

Impressionist harmony treats a chord as a colour, not a step toward the next one: shapes slide in parallel, the bass holds still while harmonies drift above, scales without a leading tone (whole-tone, pentatonic, the church modes) take away the pull home, and ninths and elevenths become places to rest.
