Sampler A/B

The same BRR banks through the sampler before and after the playback fixes. Before: the browser resamples each 12–16 kHz sample with linear interpolation, and a stolen voice is cut instantly (8 voices). After: each sample is upsampled once at load with a windowed sinc, and stolen voices fade over 5 ms (16 voices). The stored bank and its onchain cost are unchanged.
