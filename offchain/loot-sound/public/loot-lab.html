Loot Dungeon Sound Lab

Every track the 30 Loot Dungeon Beasts play. Species Songs and Name Songs come from the Beasts composer unchanged. Sound Checks are new: the Beast's whole band warms up for ten bars in its key (an opening, three drills drawn from nine, a close), and every take is another routine. Rounds are new, a candidate to replace the Species Song's canon: the harmony comes first (a chord loop in the Beast's mode, in 4/4, 6/8 or 3/4 by family), one line is written over it for each voice, the lines end to end are the theme, and every voice plays that theme exactly, one loop apart. Each line has a shape and opens with the motif's rhythm, lines that step down across a chord change may hold over it as suspensions, and after a sequence canon in the middle the round comes back over a second loop. Monthly Track Editions are new: the edition engine with chromatic harmony after Realm of Common Tones (mode mixture, chromatic mediants, common-tone pivots) and Kingdom of Sweets (applied dominants, diminished sevenths, the Neapolitan and German sixth). Sound effects: regular or mega. Instruments: a ten-instrument sample bank (harp, marimba, glockenspiel, strings, cello, flute, pizzicato, clarinet, timpani, piano) at 16 kHz, with the TinyChip presets one click away. Beast ids, tiers and families are placeholders until the final list.
