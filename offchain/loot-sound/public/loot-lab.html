Loot Dungeon Sound Lab

Every track the 30 Loot Dungeon Beasts play. Species Songs and Name Songs come from the Beasts composer unchanged. Sound Checks are new: the Beast's whole band warms up for ten bars in its key (an opening, three drills drawn from nine, a close), and every take is another routine. Rounds are new, a candidate to replace the Species Song's canon: the harmony comes first (a chord loop in the Beast's mode, in 4/4, 6/8 or 3/4 by family), one line is written over it for each voice, the lines end to end are the theme, and every voice plays that theme exactly, one loop apart. Each line has a shape and opens with the motif's rhythm, lines that step down across a chord change may hold over it as suspensions, and after a sequence canon in the middle the round comes back over a second loop. Monthly Track Editions are new: the edition engine with chromatic harmony after Realm of Common Tones (mode mixture, chromatic mediants, common-tone pivots) and Kingdom of Sweets (applied dominants, diminished sevenths, the Neapolitan and German sixth). Sound effects: regular or mega. Instruments: FM patches, built in the page when it loads (flute, ocarina, clarinet, oboe, bassoon, strings and a lead violin after Schottstaedt's FM violin, bowed cello, trumpet, trombones, horn, choir, two organs, a pad, electric piano, vibraphone, guitar, kalimba, koto, harp, marimba, glockenspiel, pizzicato, three basses and drums), with the TinyChip presets one click away. Each track picks its band from a pool for its style, by Beast, edition and purchase, and the band changes as the track drifts. Editions here also try: an intro on half the purchases (at most four and a half seconds), a harmony on a tune's repeat, a second lead on some B or C tunes, drum fills and cuts, a walking bass on three purchases in five, slower styles often taken faster, and three faster styles (Dungeon Run in 6/8, Crypt Dash, Skull Punk). Rounds have drums whose groove and fills move with drift. Name Songs and Rounds are played higher than written (an octave, a fifth).
